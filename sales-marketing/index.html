Services

Sales & Marketing

Driving real estate sales. Elevating investment growth.

TREG makes property sales seamless by combining market insight, targeted marketing and personal client service.

We maximise exposure for every property and guide buyers and sellers through a smooth, transparent transaction.

Sales services

  • Market research and positioning

    TREG analyses local demand, pricing trends and buyer demographics to position each project around its lifestyle benefits and long-term value.

  • Branding and messaging

    We craft compelling narratives through professional visuals, listings and tours that show what sets each project apart.

  • Lead generation

    We drive interest through targeted digital advertising, search and property platforms, and through open houses and partnerships with brokers and relocation specialists.

  • Sales conversion

    TREG guides clients through negotiations, financing options and contracts, with the support of legal and financial partners, for a smooth and transparent closing.

TREG connects investors with high-value real estate opportunities through clear, trust-driven fund marketing. With tailored outreach, transparent reporting and ongoing investor support, we help clients grow and diversify their portfolios with confidence.

Marketing services

  • Market and investor research

    TREG defines target investor groups such as high-net-worth individuals, institutions and family offices, and identifies market trends, risk appetite and return expectations.

  • Fund branding and messaging

    TREG creates professional fund materials, including pitch decks, reports and fact sheets, that set out the structure, projected returns and advantages of each fund.

  • Investor engagement

    TREG engages potential investors through one-on-one consultations, detailed investment models, case studies and transparent, compliance-driven communication.

  • Capital raising and conversion

    TREG presents opportunities with clear risk and return, streamlines subscription and supports onboarding and documentation.

  • Investor relations

    TREG builds long-term investor confidence with transparent quarterly updates and relationships that encourage reinvestment and referrals.

Track record

Between 2005 and 2019 TREG marketed 120+ projects in 12 countries to buyers in the Gulf, for developers including Berkeley, Barratt London, SevenCapital, Tridel and Inanlar.
