Exhibition & Event Management

Unmatched experience in world-class exhibitions and events

With over 20 years of proven expertise through our legacy firm, Top Expo, TREG brings unmatched experience in delivering world-class exhibitions and marketing campaigns.

We guide clients through every phase, from strategy and promotion to execution and post-event optimisation, for strong visibility, meaningful connections and measurable results.

What we do

  • Planning and strategy

    TREG develops exhibition strategies tailored to client objectives, target audiences and market positioning.

  • Pre-event promotion

    We build momentum before the event through targeted campaigns, invitations and digital outreach.

  • On-site execution

    Our team delivers world-class exhibition experiences, from booth design to visitor engagement and lead generation.

  • Post-event follow-up

    TREG makes every connection count by managing follow-ups, nurturing leads and turning event contacts into long-term relationships.

  • Evaluation and optimisation

    We analyse performance and outcomes to refine the strategy and deliver stronger results with each exhibition.
